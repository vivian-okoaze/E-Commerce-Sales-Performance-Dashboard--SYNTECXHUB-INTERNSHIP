E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

An in-depth view of total sales, profit, product performance, and growth rate from 2022 to 2024



📌 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a multi-dimensional analysis of business performance across three fiscal years (2022–2024). It is designed to help stakeholders quickly identify trends, evaluate product performance, assess regional sales distribution, and monitor profit growth over time.

 📂 Dashboard Sections

1. 📈 Monthly Sales Trend by Year
Tracks monthly sales across 2022, 2023, and 2024 using a clustered bar chart.

- 2023 consistently outperformed other years, peaking at **0.39M in August**
- A mid-year dip (May–July) is observed across all three years, indicating seasonal slowdown
- 2024 shows a strong year-end recovery, reaching **0.38M in December**


2. 🍩 Total Profit by Year
A donut chart displaying the profit share across the three years.


| Year | Total Profit | Share |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 2023 | 666.87K | 36.15% |
| 2022 | 604.94K | 32.79% |
| 2024 | 572.86K | 31.05% |

> 2023 was the most profitable year, though the gap between years remains relatively narrow.

 4. 🗺️ Total Sales by Region and Category
A stacked bar chart comparing sales across four regions — West, East, South, and North — broken down by category (Accessories, Electronics, Office).

- **West** leads all regions with Electronics contributing **1.46M**
- **North** is the weakest region across all categories
- **Electronics** is the dominant category in every region
- **Office supplies** consistently represent the smallest category share


5. 📉 Total Profit by Month
A line chart visualizing monthly profit fluctuations throughout the year.

- **Lowest point:** February at **119K**
- **Highest point:** December at **174K**
- Profit is highly volatile, with a clear **Q1 weakness and Q4 strength** pattern


 6. 📊 Total Sales by Year Quarter
A waterfall/line chart showing quarterly sales from 2022 Q1 down to 2024 Q3.

- **2023 Q1** recorded the all-time high at **1,050K**
- A gradual decline follows post-2023 peak
- Trend indicates 2023 was the business's strongest year overall


 📈 Growth Rate Summary (2022–2024)

💡 Recommendations

1. **Address Q1 Weakness** — Launch targeted promotions in January–February to offset the consistent profit dip
2. **Boost North Region** — Investigate underperformance in the North and apply strategies from the West region's success
3. **Sustain 2024 Recovery** — Leverage the December momentum to set a strong baseline for 2025
4. **Expand Electronics** — Given its dominance across all regions, increasing the Electronics product range could further drive revenue
